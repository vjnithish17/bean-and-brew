ABOUT BEAN & BREW

Our Story

Founded with an enduring love for deep aromas and genuine human connection.

Bean & Brew began a decade ago with a vintage roaster and a dream: to transform coffee from a morning routine into a sensory sanctuary. We travel the globe to partner directly with generational farmers who harvest beans at their sweetest ripeness.

Every batch is roasted in micro-lots right here in our workshop, balancing nuanced floral notes, caramel sweetness, and silky finishes. Whether you need a tranquil corner to write or a warm haven to catch up with friends, our cafe is your home away from home.
